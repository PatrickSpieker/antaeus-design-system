# Brand assets

[Index](../index.md)

| Asset | Files | Intended use |
|---|---|---|
| Open arch mark | [SVG](../assets/logo-mark.svg), [PNG](../assets/logo-mark.png) | Light bone surfaces |
| Inverse arch mark | [SVG](../assets/logo-mark-inverse.svg), [PNG](../assets/logo-mark-inverse.png) | Dark plum surfaces |
| Antaeus Health wordmark | [SVG](../assets/logo-wordmark.svg) | Mark and full brand name |

These are the existing founder-supplied mark assets; SVGs were redrawn to match the raster originals. Preserve proportions and use the inverse mark on dark surfaces. Do not treat asset colors as new interface tokens.

The wordmark SVG contains live text referencing IBM Plex Sans with an Arial/sans-serif fallback; it is not outlined. Its appearance depends on font availability in the rendering context. Consuming products must prepare their platform asset accordingly. Native iOS consumers can use the PNG marks or convert the SVGs through their asset tooling.

Font files, screenshots, prototypes, and reference implementations are not published.
