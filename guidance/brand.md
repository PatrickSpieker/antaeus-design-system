# Brand and voice

[Index](../index.md)

Antaeus Health is a consumer health platform. Members own their records, track symptoms and measurements, coordinate care, and understand their health. The name comes from the Greek giant who drew strength from contact with the earth: health begins with a person's body, data, and decisions.

The product is serious when needed, calm and delightful otherwise. It is neither an administrative patient portal nor a superficial wellness app. Its surfaces include a consumer mobile app, member web tools, and marketing.

## Design posture

Use generous whitespace, deep plum accents, warm bone surfaces, muted semantic colors, and restrained motion. Serif typography creates occasional editorial moments; sans-serif supports the interface; monospace supports metrics. Avoid decorative clutter, emoji in product chrome, bluish-purple gradients, and generic ornamental motifs.

## Writing

- Be direct, warm, literate, and plainspoken. Explain without overexplaining.
- Address the reader as “you”; call people “members” internally and in marketing, not “users” or “patients”.
- Prefer present tense and active voice.
- Use sentence case for headings and labels. Buttons use sentence-case verbs.
- Small eyebrow labels may use uppercase with 8% letter spacing; proper nouns retain their capitalization.
- Translate clinical terms on first use without erasing the clinical name.
- No emoji in product chrome. A single intentional editorial glyph may appear in an empty state or celebration; do not use glyphs as interface icons.

## Numbers and dates

Use monospace with tabular numerals for data and metrics. Separate measurements from their units with a thin space, use an en dash for ranges, spell out the month in conversational dates, and use ISO dates in dense tables.

## Imagery

Use warm, desaturated documentary imagery. Marketing may use black-and-white photography, with light photographic grain. Do not add decorative noise or texture to interface surfaces. Avoid clinical stock photos, teal-orange grading, full-bleed photography in product chrome, and hand-drawn interface illustrations.

## Brand language

“Health, finally aimed at you.” “Your body, your record, your decisions.” “The ground beneath your health.” “Care that answers to you.”
